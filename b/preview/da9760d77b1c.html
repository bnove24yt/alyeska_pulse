What moving up would mean

Your next band, Strong, starts at 75. Statewide, businesses at 90 or better have added Google reviews at about 3 times the rate of businesses under 40 since July.

  • Ask at the moment it goes well. A card on the check, a line on the receipt, a word from the server. Businesses that ask add reviews several times faster than those that wait, and the ones that come unasked skew unhappy.
  • Answer every review inside a day. Platforms rank responsive businesses higher and readers trust them more. A calm reply to a bad review is read by a hundred people who never see the review itself.
  • Close the gap between the room and the door. If dine-in and take-out disagree, the fix is usually packaging, timing or the pickup counter, not the kitchen. That is the cheapest score to move.
  • Keep the listing true. Wrong hours, a dead phone number or an old menu photo produce one-star reviews that have nothing to do with the food.
