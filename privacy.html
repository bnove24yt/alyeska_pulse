Privacy

Draft for review · September 18, 2026

Who we are

Pulse is operated by Aurora Capital LLC, doing business as Alyeska Pulse, Anchorage, Alaska. Questions about this policy go to hello@alyeskapulse.com.

What we collect

Public business data. For every licensed restaurant, bar, coffee stand and shop in Alaska we record the business name, address, category, and the star rating and review count shown on public platforms (Google, Yelp, TripAdvisor, OpenTable, DoorDash and others we add). We record ratings and counts only. We do not store review text or reviewer identities.

What you give us when you claim a business. Your name, role, work email and mobile number, the business you're claiming, and the code and link confirmations used to verify you. If a claim needs a manual look, anything you send to support that claim (for example a photo of a business license) is kept with the claim.

Ordinary web data. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) records standard request logs, including IP address and browser type, for security and to keep the site running. We don't run advertising trackers.

How we use it

To verify that you're entitled to see a business's page; to show you that page; to email you a sign-in link when you ask for one and a monthly note when the score updates; to answer you when you write to us; and to keep the service secure. We don't sell your contact details and we don't share them with third parties except the services that send our email and text messages on our behalf.

Claiming a business on Pulse does not put you on a marketing list for any other product. If we ever want to tell you about something other than Pulse, we'll ask first.

Who can see what

A business's score and details are shown only to people who have claimed that business and been verified, and to anyone they add. They are not public, not shared with other businesses, and not indexed by search engines. The public lookup shows only a business's name, town, category, when we began tracking it, and how many platforms feed it.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the monthly email with the link in any message. You can ask us to remove your claim and your contact details at any time by emailing hello@alyeskapulse.com; we'll do it within ten days. The public business data stays, because it's public.

Where it lives

Pulse is run from Anchorage, Alaska. Data is stored on servers in the United States.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll email claimed owners before it takes effect.
